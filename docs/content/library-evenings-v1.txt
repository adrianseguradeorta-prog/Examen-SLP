Last month, our community library tried opening until eight on Tuesday and Thursday evenings. We expected more people to borrow books after work. In fact, the study spaces were busy, while the service desk was often quiet. Some visitors told us they needed a warm, quiet place to prepare for courses, rather than help finding a book. Next month, we will keep both late evenings, but a staffed desk will be available only on Thursday. On Tuesday, visitors can use the study area and the self-service machines. This is not a permanent decision. We will review the arrangement after six weeks and ask users who might be left out, including people who need help with the machines. A quiet desk does not necessarily mean an empty building. The trial has shown us something useful, but counting visitors alone will not tell us whether the service works for everyone.
